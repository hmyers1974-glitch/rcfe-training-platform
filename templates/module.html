Key Leadership Reminder

When a resident is found on the floor, slow the situation down. Protect the resident from additional injury, assess before moving them when appropriate, obtain emergency assistance when indicated, and direct staff clearly. Documentation and required notifications are part of the response — not an afterthought.

This training scenario is educational and should be applied together with your facility's policies, emergency procedures, resident care plan, and applicable California requirements.
