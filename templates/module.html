Episode 1

Resident Down

It is 8:05 AM. Your morning has just started.

Before you can settle into your office, one of your caregivers approaches you quickly.

8:05 AM · RESIDENT CARE HALLWAY

Maria is moving quickly toward you. Her expression is serious. You can tell immediately that something has happened.

Maria

Caregiver

Morning Shift

MARIA
“Heather, I walked into Mrs. Smith’s room and observed her beside the bed on the floor.”

Maria is waiting for your direction. What do you say?

YOU
“Maria, don't move her. Come with me.”

You walk quickly toward Mrs. Smith's room.

As you approach the doorway, you focus on what Maria actually told you.

Maria said she observed Mrs. Smith beside the bed on the floor.

She did not say that she saw Mrs. Smith fall.

That distinction matters.

A strong Administrator responds to the immediate risk without turning an assumption into a fact.

Now You Are the Administrator

As you head toward Mrs. Smith's room, several things are competing for your attention.

What is your first responsibility?

Episode 2

The Blank MAR

Before you reach Mrs. Smith's room, another employee approaches.

MEDICATION TECHNICIAN

“Heather, I found a blank MAR entry from last night. I don't know whether the medication was given.”

You now have two active issues.

Which issue comes first, what facts do you have, and what still needs to be verified?

This is where the R.E.A.L. Administrator method begins.
